Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about VideoSnap.

What is VideoSnap?

VideoSnap is a platform that allows users to upload, organize, and manage videos in one place.

Do I need an account to use VideoSnap?

You can browse public pages without an account, but you'll need to sign up to upload and manage videos.

Is VideoSnap free to use?

Yes. The basic features are free. Additional premium features may be introduced in the future.

How can I reset my password?

Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the Login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

How do I contact support?

Visit the Contact Us page and submit your query using the contact form.

Which video formats are supported?

Popular video formats such as MP4, MOV, AVI, and MKV are supported.

Can I delete my uploaded videos?

Yes. After logging in, you can manage and delete your videos from the "My Videos" page.
